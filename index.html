FAQ

Find answers to your questions

How do I create and configure my race?

Set start times, distances, and timed/untimed events from your dashboard under Preview → Edit → Registration in the Events section.

How do cloning and annual race updates work?

Build your race once. Each year, clone the event to launch next year's race instantly — same structure, fresh dates.

Can Run The Day help first-time race directors?

Absolutely. First-time races are our specialty. Think of us as your race-day co-pilot — you handle the vision, we help you execute.

Why can't people register for my race?

Usually a gap between pricing tier dates or you've hit capacity. Check both under Preview → Edit → Registration.

Can I update my race after publishing?

Yes — race info, images, sponsors, and waivers. The only thing you cannot change is the race URL.

What does the organizer pay?

Run The Day is free to the organizer. Runners pay a transparent 5–7% + $1 transaction fee.

Are there monthly platform fees?

No monthly fees for organizers. You keep costs predictable while we handle the technology.

What can races expect from the RTD team on race day?

A full timing team — not just equipment. Chip setup, mats, bib assignment, live results, finish-line displays, and on-site support from arrival.

Do you support DIY timing?

We specialize in professional chip timing with humans on site. Ask us during your demo which option fits your event size.
