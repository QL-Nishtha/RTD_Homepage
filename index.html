FAQ

Find answers to your questions

How do I create and configure my race?

Set start times, distances, and timed/untimed events from your dashboard: Preview → Edit → Registration → Events.

How do cloning and annual race updates work?

Build your race once. Each year, clone the event to launch next year’s race instantly with sponsors and structure intact.

Can Run The Day help first-time race directors?

Absolutely. First-time races are our specialty — setup, planning, timelines, and race-day workflow so nothing gets missed.

Why can’t people register for my race?

Usually a gap between pricing tier dates or you’ve hit capacity. Check Preview → Edit → Registration → Events.

Can I update the race after publishing?

Yes — info, images, sponsors, waivers, and more. The only thing you cannot change is the race URL.

What does the organizer pay?

Registration software is free to the organizer. Runners pay a small transaction fee (typically 5–7% + $1). Timing and full-ops packages are custom.

What’s included with chip timing?

A full race-day timing team: setup, equipment and mats, bib assignment, live results, finish-line displays, and on-site support.

Do you help with marketing?

Yes — email and text messaging plus marketing help to grow your race, tailored for fields under 5,000 and series events up to 10,000.

Can we run fundraising teams?

Easy fundraising teams are built in — collect team info at registration and keep cause dollars flowing to your organization.

Is support human?

Support from humans (no bots). We’re your race-day co-pilot — you handle the vision, we help you execute.
