Operations

The questions directors
ask us first

01 · Timing redundancy

What happens if a timing mat fails?

Every timer handles this differently. At Run The Day, we run backup systems with on-the-ground oversight to ensure all times are captured.

02 · Packet pickup

Who runs it, and how many volunteers?

Our team can run it, or your volunteers can. The check-in app is so easy an 83-year-old recently used it — and the average volunteer assigns 100 bibs an hour, so you need fewer people than you think.

03 · Live results

How fast do results and awards post?

In real time, onto the results page. You'll see custom age-group awards live, so you can announce them before runners get antsy and leave.
